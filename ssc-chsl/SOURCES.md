# SSC CHSL Tier-I Mock Sources and Notes

## Exam pattern used

The interactive practice uses four sections of 25 questions each, 15 minutes per section, 100 questions, 200 maximum marks, +2 for a correct answer, and a 0.50-mark deduction for an incorrect answer. Section time is not transferable. Check the latest SSC notification before an actual exam because SSC can revise its pattern.

## References consulted

- [Staff Selection Commission (official website)](https://ssc.gov.in/) — official notices, examination calendar, answer keys, and candidate resources. Prefer the current CHSL notice for final rules.
- [Testbook: SSC CHSL Previous Year Papers](https://testbook.com/ssc-chsl/previous-year-papers) — lists shift-wise Tier-I papers, including 2025, 2024, 2023, and earlier papers; its pattern summary reports 100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes, and negative marking.
- [Adda247: SSC CHSL Previous Year Question Papers](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-chsl-previous-year-question-paper/) — lists year/shift papers and describes the four-section, 15-minute-per-section format.

## Question provenance

The interactive bank contains 1,000 original practice items generated across 10 papers. The questions are written in a PYQ-style using the topics and patterns identified in the subject syllabus and publicly listed papers. They are not presented as verbatim official PYQs, and paid or copyrighted paper text has not been copied into this resource.

General Awareness questions focus on stable static facts. Review current SSC notices and a current-affairs source separately for time-sensitive facts.
